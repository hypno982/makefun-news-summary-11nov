World news, Wednesday 11 November 2026

Ukraine: A second day of US-mediated talks between Kyiv and Moscow ran four hours in Abu Dhabi. President Volodymyr Zelensky, who did not attend, called them "constructive and quite substantive"; Secretary of State Marco Rubio, speaking in Paris, called the meeting "very productive" (BBC). Hours earlier Russian strikes wounded nine people and damaged Ukraine's power network (BBC).

United States: The House voted to refer articles of impeachment against Defence Secretary Pete Hegseth to a committee, effectively killing the effort by Republican congressman Thomas Massie (BBC). In Mississippi, Democrat Scott Colom was projected to win a Senate seat long held by Republicans, unseating Cindy Hyde-Smith (CBS News).

Security: The Justice Department said three Iran-backed suspects were involved in 18 attacks and attempted attacks, including a September plot near RAF Fairford, a base used by US forces in England (BBC).

Law and media: A judge allowed President Trump's $10bn (£7.5bn) defamation suit against the BBC over a Panorama edit to proceed, and dismissed a second claim under the state's unfair trade practices law (BBC).

Haiti: El Salvador's President Nayib Bukele said a joint task force destroyed the headquarters of the 400 Mawozo gang; Haiti's prime minister praised the strike (BBC).

Climate: World leaders opened the high-level segment of the COP31 summit in Antalya, Turkey (UNFCCC, AFP).

Africa, Asia, Australia: Congo's Ebola outbreak passed 11,000 cases, government data showed (Reuters). Asian markets fell after a hot US October inflation reading, Japan's Nikkei and Korea's Kospi among them (NDTV Profit). Nine people were seriously hurt when a bus crashed in Seongnam, South Korea (Yonhap). Australia marked Remembrance Day at services nationwide (Prime Minister of Australia).
